THE CFO’S VIEW

Numbers tell a story.
People give it meaning.

For Metchelyn, financial leadership begins with discipline: respect the resources, understand the details, and make every decision count.

Her approach pairs financial focus with a deep regard for the people behind the numbers. She is known for steady determination, clear standards, and a generosity of knowledge that helps a team grow in confidence as well as capability.

She believes sound stewardship should strengthen the whole picture—financially, professionally, and in the life of a community.

02THE JOURNEY

FROM DAVAO TO A LIFE OF SERVICE

Earned, step
by determined step.

A story of adaptation and purposeful work—each chapter building the perspective she brings to leadership today.

I
DAVAO, PHILIPPINES

Learning with ambition

Raised in Davao, Metchelyn was a serious student in a private school where English was the language of instruction. She grew up speaking multiple languages and learned early to meet challenge with determination.

II
A NEW START IN OHIO

Building a life from the ground up

She moved to the United States in her early twenties. Adjusting to a new culture and the realities of life in Ohio took time, but her focus, financial discipline, and persistence kept her moving forward.

III
HEALTHCARE · SERVICE

Care, earned through hard work

Through further study, she entered nursing. Her diligence and care earned the respect of doctors, colleagues, and patients—and strengthened her belief that real leadership starts by showing up for others.

IV
FINANCE · LEADERSHIP

Stewardship with a human lens

Today, as a CFO, she brings that same work ethic to financial leadership: protect what matters, use resources thoughtfully, and help teams build lasting capacity.
